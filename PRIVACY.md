# Privacy

Phonetix does not collect, sell or share any data about you. It has no account, no analytics and no advertising.

## What stays on your device

Phonetix reads the text of the pages you visit to tell which language each part is in, how its words are said and what they mean. All of that is worked out in the browser, from dictionaries and models it downloads once and keeps. When you speak to the translator, the recording is turned into text on your device and is not kept or sent anywhere.

Your settings stay in the browser's local storage: your languages, the sites you switched Phonetix off on, and how it looks and behaves.

## What it downloads

- Dictionaries, the list of translation models and the speech model, from this project's releases on GitHub (github.com/nanbiz/phonetix).
- Translation models, from Mozilla's servers, the same files Firefox uses for its own translations.
- Parts of the speech model, from Hugging Face.

These downloads ask for files by language. They do not carry anything you read or wrote.

## What it sends

When you open the card for a word, that one word is looked up on Wiktionary (en.wiktionary.org), for a pronunciation or a recording a person made. Recordings and drawings of how a sound is made come from Wikimedia Commons (commons.wikimedia.org). These requests carry no account or identifier of yours.

If you set a dictionary host of your own in the advanced settings, dictionaries and models are fetched from the address you gave instead.
